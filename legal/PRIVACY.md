# Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** 2026-09-14
**Provider:** Yumi ("the Provider")
**Project repository:** https://github.com/rsyumi/RisuNest
**Contact:** GitHub Issues in the project repository

## 1. Summary

RisuNest is software that runs on your device. The Provider does not collect, store, sell, or share information that identifies you, and the only network service the Provider operates is the Endpoint Registry described in Section 4.

## 2. What the Provider Does Not Collect

The Provider operates no accounts or logins, collects no email addresses, usage analytics, telemetry, automatic error reports, or advertising identifiers, and does not use tracking technologies. The random lookup identifier held by the Endpoint Registry is described in Section 4. Your content is not collected either: chats, characters, presets, lorebooks, prompts, and settings never reach the Provider.

The Provider does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13 (or the applicable age threshold under local law).

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

Chat history, characters, presets, lorebooks, images, and the API keys and credentials you enter are stored on your device, where the Provider cannot reach them. You delete this data yourself, either through the application's own data reset function or by uninstalling the software.

## 4. The Endpoint Registry

The Registry stores three things: a random identifier generated by your device, a single string encrypted on your device, and the time that entry was last updated. The encryption key is not sent to the Registry, so the Provider cannot decrypt what is stored. No account information, unique device identifiers, the contents of synchronized data, or chat content is sent to it.

The Registry has no authentication, and so it cannot act on a request to delete a particular entry: there is no way to confirm that the person asking is the one who registered it. Every entry is deleted automatically within 31 days of its last update.

The Provider keeps no application logs for the Registry. The Registry runs on Cloudflare infrastructure, and Cloudflare's privacy policy governs how network metadata such as IP addresses and request times is handled.

## 5. Connections to Third-Party Services

Except when communicating with GitHub to check for automatic updates, RisuNest communicates with third-party services only when you explicitly enable the relevant feature. These include AI providers, RisuAI accounts and RisuRealm, external backup and synchronization storage, model distribution hosts, and any other service you set up yourself. These connections run directly between your device and the service in question and do not pass through the Provider's servers. What is sent, and how it is handled, is governed by each service's own privacy policy; the Provider neither relays nor reads it.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

The Provider holds no information that identifies you and none of the data on your device, and therefore cannot act on requests to access, correct, export, or delete it. That data is entirely under your control.

Features you have not enabled do not communicate externally. The Endpoint Registry is contacted only when you use the server address sharing feature. An entry you registered is deleted within 31 days once you stop updating it; to stop using an identifier immediately, generate a new identifier and key.

## 7. Changes and Contact

The Provider may revise this notice. Revisions take effect when they are posted to the project repository. Questions are accepted only through GitHub Issues in the project repository.
