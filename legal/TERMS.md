## 6. Endpoint Registry

The Provider operates one minimal service, the Endpoint Registry, which lets you pass a server address from one of your devices to another. Its only function is to store a single encrypted string under an identifier generated by your device and to return that string when the same identifier is presented.

Encryption takes place on your device. The encryption key is not sent to the Registry, so the Provider cannot decrypt what is stored. The Registry has no accounts and no authentication: anyone who knows an identifier can read or overwrite the entry stored under it, and there is no proof of ownership and no recovery. Entries are deleted within 31 days of their last update.

The Registry runs on Cloudflare infrastructure, and Cloudflare's terms and policies apply to that part of the Service. It is provided free of charge with no guarantee of availability or data retention, and the Provider may change or discontinue it without notice. You must not use it as a general-purpose data store or as a means of relaying traffic.
